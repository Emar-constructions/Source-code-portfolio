Room 04 — Method

The 45-day build.

If a milestone slips, you hear it from us first — with the reason and a revised date in writing. The daily photo audits don't stop, and neither does the accountability.

Days 01—07

Scan & lock

The floorplate is mapped with optical LiDAR to sub-millimetre accuracy. HVAC routes, conduit runs and floor levels are catalogued before a single wall is touched — and the bill of quantities is locked at 0% escalation.

  • Photorealistic 3D walkthrough & material board
  • Itemised BOQ, guaranteed 0% price escalation
  • Direct WhatsApp channel with the founder & lead engineers
Days 08—18

Civil & MEP

Masonry modifications, plumbing pressure-tested at 10.2 bar for 48 hours, and fire-retardant FRLS copper cabling routed through heavy-duty conduits — with zero tampering of load-bearing RCC.

  • Hydrostatic pressure certificate issued
  • Dedicated circuits for commercial & appliance loads
  • Daily photo audit logged to your phone
Days 19—30

Factory joinery

Every carcass, storage unit and display case is CNC-cut in our facility from 710-grade calibrated BWP marine plywood, finished in 1mm European laminates on German soft-close hardware — dry-fitted before dispatch.

  • Certified 100% waterproof BWP 710 board
  • Lifetime-warranty hardware, factory dry-fit check
Days 31—40

Finishes & light

Stone pre-laid at the warehouse for flawless grain symmetry, fluted timber acoustic cladding fitted, and CRI 92+ warm lighting calibrated — not a single visible wire at the end.

  • Stone grain symmetry sign-off
  • Acoustic & glare-free lighting test
  • Joinery margins held under 0.5mm
Days 41—45

Handover

A 140-point audit covering sealants, electrical loads and hinge dampening. Industrial HEPA clean, then keys and your 10-year warranty deed — on or before day 45, as written.

  • Signed 140-point quality report
  • 10-year comprehensive warranty deed
  • Handover strictly on or before Day 45

Room 06 — Questions

Asked before every project.

Straight answers to the things clients usually only ask after the contract. Ask us anything else — Aquib answers his own phone.

Q—01Do you handle both the design and the construction of a project?+

Yes — that is the whole point of Emar. We are a design-and-execution practice: interiors and construction sit at the same table from day one. One team surveys, draws, builds and hands over, so nothing is lost between the drawing and the building.

Q—02Which areas do you serve?+

Our home base is Chickmaglur, Karnataka 577101, with active project delivery across Bengaluru and the wider Karnataka coffee belt — Sakleshpur, Hassan, Mudigere and surrounding towns. Coffee-estate villas and homestays are a speciality.

Q—03How much does a turnkey interior or construction project cost?+

We quote only from a measured, itemised bill of quantities — never a guess. As an honest orientation: straightforward fit-outs start around ₹1,800 per sq.ft, premium interiors ₹2,400–2,900 per sq.ft, and bespoke architectural work ₹3,200 and above. The real number is locked after a free site survey and 3D design.

Q—04How does the 45-day handover guarantee work?+

Every project runs on a dated milestone schedule that is written into the contract. You receive a daily photo audit of the site on WhatsApp, and if a milestone slips, you hear it from us first — with the reason and a revised date in writing.

Q—05What warranty do I get?+

A 10-year structural and hardware warranty deed, handed to you with the keys at handover. Because the same team that designed the space also built it, accountability never changes hands.

Q—06How do I start a project with Emar?+

Call or WhatsApp Aquib on +91 91874 05146. We visit the site, take measurements, walk you through a 3D design and lock the BOQ — and only then does the 45-day clock start.
